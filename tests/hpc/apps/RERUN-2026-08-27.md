(GROMACS), `peer_rank_resolvable`, `halo_peer_fanout` (LAMMPS),
`ucx_peer_matrix_is_recoverable` (QE, npool=2 arm).

**2 of 2 predicted holds held.** GROMACS's `entity_close_accounted` and

**The uncertain one resolved honestly.** `verbs_is_internode_only` is now

**One apparent miss, which is not one.** I predicted

## The headline: the first HPC-X rank matrix this project has produced

LAMMPS, `comm_edges`, where there were previously **zero rows**:

```
transport  unit             edges  sends   bytes
ucx        datatype-units      64   6,325  408,922,853
```

64 edges is the full 8×8. `unresolved_ucx = 0`; every other `link_health`
finding 0. The heaviest edges are symmetric neighbour pairs at ~19.8 MB —
`comm_brick`'s halo exchange, in rank space.

Nothing was invented: the study measured "51 MB and ~800 messages per rank" at
the UCX layer, and 8 × 51 MB = 408 MB, 8 × 790 = 6,320. The same traffic, now
attributable.

## GROMACS: an inference became a measurement

`ucx_tag_send.device` on the two arms of `GMX_ENABLE_DIRECT_GPU_COMM`:

```
ON                                  OFF
device 3   9,947 sends  31.74 GB    device 65535  103,215 sends  72.56 GB
device 0   5,880        11.97 GB    (nothing else)
device 1   5,880        11.98 GB
device 2   5,880        11.94 GB
host      75,705         4.93 GB
```

27% of sends by count carry 93% of the bytes and come from device memory, one
ordinal per GPU; with the knob off every buffer is host. Small control traffic
stays on the host either way.

And the isolation is exact: rank 7 / device 3 is **7,007 sends, 25,662,580,944
units**, which is the study's predicted PME-rank volume `12 B x 2,136,412 x
1001` **to the byte**.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT OVERTURN. The study concluded "UCX never used GPUDirect
RDMA — it pipelined through host memory, and the 19% is won on GROMACS's side of
the NIC". That still stands. `device_of(buffer)` answers *where the
application's buffer lived*, not *whether the NIC read it directly*; a device
pointer in with host registrations at the NIC is exactly "UCX accepted a device
pointer and staged it". What changed is that both halves are now visible at
once, where the study had to infer the application side from registration counts
because the record carried no memory type at all.

## Two new findings

**A. QE's peer join fails on the ENDPOINT side, and `unresolved_ucx` caught it.**

```
qe-npool2: 27,505 tag_sends   8 endpoints   8 ep_connections   4 worker_addresses
           6 comm_edges, of which 4 have dst_rank = -1
           link_health: unresolved_ucx = 25,005   (91% of sends)
```

Item 13 fixed the *worker* side and QE's `ucx_worker_address` is populated now —
that is why `ucx_peer_matrix_is_recoverable` flipped. But 91% of its sends still
resolve to no peer, because most of its endpoints carry no dialled peer address:
`UcxEpConnection` is emitted only when `UCP_EP_PARAM_FIELD_REMOTE_ADDRESS` is
actually set, and an endpoint created from a socket address or a connection
request has none. Deliberate — a row of zeroes there would read as a measured
null peer — but it leaves a class of application unattributable.

This is a genuinely different gap from item 13 and did not exist as a visible
number until today: without the `unresolved_ucx` row added with the UCX lane,
this capture would have reported a clean bill of health over a 91% hole. Raised
as todos item 18.

**B. `03_quantumespresso/check.sh` misreports derived tables as empty.**

It loads `<csv>/*.csv` into sqlite. `comm_edges` is a DERIVED table and lives in
the parquet the `link` step writes, so there is no `comm_edges.csv` to import
and the runner reported "comm_edges is EMPTY — unanswerable" for a table that
exists and has six rows. Three of its `[skip]` lines are this, not a property of
the capture. The other three studies read parquet and are unaffected.

Left as-is here, deliberately: it is the study's own runner, the finding is
recorded, and rewriting another agent's harness is not this file's job.

## What still cannot be answered for QE

`ucx_and_verbs_agree_on_the_peer_set` is `[skip]` on both arms and should stay
that way. On the default transport pcomm sees zero wire traffic for QE because
`rc_mlx5` bypasses verbs for send and connect, so the two layers are not
describing the same traffic and the assertion is unanswerable rather than false.
That is a fact about the transport, unchanged by anything here.
